# Code of Conduct

StructureClaw contributors are expected to keep the project welcoming, technical, and respectful.

## Our Standards

Good participation includes:

- being respectful when discussing engineering tradeoffs
- keeping feedback focused on code, docs, behavior, and user impact
- giving enough context for bug reports and review comments
- making room for both English and Chinese contributors
- avoiding personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or targeted personal attacks
- publishing private information without permission
- deliberately derailing technical discussions
- using project spaces to promote spam or unrelated services

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block accounts; or take other reasonable moderation actions when behavior harms the project community.

If you see a problem, contact the maintainers privately with links and context. Please do not escalate sensitive interpersonal issues in public threads.
